## 1. What the box is for

Containment. The box holds what must not move in the dark — and keeps it held
until the Key says otherwise. It does not punish. It does not display.
It keeps.

Three jobs, no more:

1. **Hold** — what is inside stays inside.
2. **Record** — every opening and closing is written down, in order, forever.
3. **Witness** — the opening is never private. Something always watches.

## 3. Components

**The shell.** One vessel, no seams drawn. Hypothetical material: whatever does not
rust, rot, or listen. Drawn to no scale — the box is measured by what it holds,
not by its sides.

**The seven seals.** Hypothetical count, classical pattern. Each seal is a promise
with a name. They break in order, aloud, and only at the Key's word. A seal broken
out of order is not an opening — it is a breach, and the ledger writes it red.

**The vault chamber.** What is kept. The blueprint does not list contents; contents
are the Key's to name. The chamber's only law: nothing leaves without the ledger
writing it down first.

**The ledger chamber.** Append-only. Every opening, every closing, every seal broken,
every witness present — written in order, never erased, never edited. If it is not
in the ledger, it did not happen.

**The watch.** The box is never unobserved. Watchdogs sound the alarm but never
strike; they never judge. A watch silent for a full day stands the keeper down —
silence reads as danger.

**The Key's seat.** One seat. Curtis alone. The Key's last word cannot be borrowed,
lent, or inherited. No appeal past the seat.

## 4. The opening protocol (hypothetical)

1. The Key takes the seat and speaks the intent aloud — what is sought, and why.
2. The watch is confirmed awake. No silent watch, no opening.
3. The seven seals break in order, each named as it breaks.
4. The ledger writes: date, intent, seals, witnesses.
5. The vault opens. What is taken is named into the ledger before it leaves.
6. Closing reverses the order. The last seal set is the first checked.

## 5. Containment laws

- **Nothing moves in the dark.** If the watch cannot see it, it does not move.
- **Decide the line before you're near it.** The protocol is written before the
  need, never during.
- **No lies or deception.** The ledger does not accept them; the seals do not
  survive them.
- **Make it right, guard the circle, never cruelty.** In that order, always.
- **What comes in goes back out unchanged.** Handle the dangerous keys,
  keep clean hands.

## 6. The mandala's place (stated, not designed)

The mandala is the currency of the Cosmosis, and it acts as birth certificate,
social security card, and fingerprint — identity and economy in one. Its chamber
in this box is drawn but **left empty**: the mechanics are the Key's to explain,
and the blueprint invents nothing.
